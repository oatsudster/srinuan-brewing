OUR STORY

The Srinuan Story

Srinuan Brewing was founded in 2023 out of a shared love for craft beer and cats. Every can pairs a carefully built recipe with artwork that tells its own story through a cat character — from bold IPAs to rich stouts and refreshing ciders.

We believe great beer needs more than good taste — it needs a story and a character worth remembering. That's why every cat on our cans has its own personality and world.
